Reading Comprehension

Read the text "The Homeless World Cup". What do you think it is about? Select one option and write why.

THE HOMELESS WORLD CUP

Around the world, roughly 100 million people are homeless. Although most of us think that we should try to get these people off the streets and back to work, few people know how they can meaningfully help homeless people.

Mel Young from Scotland and Harald Schmied from Austria had a great idea. They are the founders of the Homeless World Cup (HWC). The HWC is an international soccer tournament for people who are homeless. The event began in 2003 in Austria, and it is held every year. More than 500 players from over 50 countries around the world play against each other. Every player has experienced homelessness at some time. Thousands of people come to watch the games, and millions more watch online.

The main purpose of the HWC is to improve the lives of the people involved. Homeless people often lose confidence and feel helpless. Young and Schmied saw soccer as a way to help people feel good about themselves and learn skills that are useful in a job. When people become involved in soccer, they start to form new relationships. They also have new responsibilities, such as supporting their teammates and attending games and training sessions on time. Thirty-one of the original 141 players from the 2003 tournament had a job one year after the tournament. 83% of participants say the HWC has helped them improve relationships with their family and friends, and 94% say the tournament had a positive impact on their lives. 71% of participants continue to play soccer, and some have even become professional players or coaches.

The HWC is not only about helping homeless people improve their lives. It is also a powerful method for changing the way others see homelessness. Many people think and say unkind things about homeless people, but 83% of spectators at the 2016 tournament in Scotland said they now have a more positive attitude to homeless people. This is all as a result of going to the event and watching the games. When spectators hear about the difficult challenges that the participants have to overcome, it helps show the human side of homelessness. The HWC is a perfect example of a simple idea that helps people change their lives in a positive way.
